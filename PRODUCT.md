# KARGO Product Brief

## Product purpose
KARGO is a pasabuy order-fulfillment workspace for buyers claiming items from seller-run travel batches and for sellers coordinating claims, payments, and fulfillment.

## Primary users
The primary users are Filipino buyers who need confidence around deadlines, payment proof, seller credibility, and order status, and independent sellers who need a compact operational view of demand, payment verification, and fulfillment progress.

## Core jobs to be done
Buyers need to discover live batches, understand the total cost and reservation deadline, claim an item, submit payment evidence, and recover a clear next action when a payment or claim needs attention. Sellers need to publish batches, review claims, verify payments, communicate trust signals, and move orders through fulfillment stages without losing context.

## Operating context
KARGO is used in short, frequent sessions on desktop and mobile. The interface must remain scannable under time pressure: deadlines, payment states, stock, and next actions should be visible before secondary details. The product uses Philippine peso amounts, social-account credibility references, and BIR verification language.

## Voice and trust posture
The voice is warm, direct, and operational. Copy should explain what happens next rather than overpromise. Trust indicators are evidence references, not guarantees. Warnings should be specific and actionable; destructive actions should state their consequence before confirmation.

## Design priorities
1. Make urgency legible without making the interface feel alarmist.
2. Use hierarchy and whitespace instead of nested cards or decorative gradients.
3. Keep buyer and seller role context visible at all times.
4. Make status, amount, and deadline values easy to scan and compare.
5. Preserve accessible focus states, touch targets, responsive tables, and reduced-motion behavior.
