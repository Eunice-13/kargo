## Mapping strategy

Mappings live in [batches.ts](../src/data/batches.ts): batch IDs map to destination covers, and exact product names map to local product files under [public/catalog](../public/catalog). Identical named product occurrences may reuse their photograph; different named products never share one. Repeated destinations reuse their destination photograph.

Representative photographs within the same named brand/product family are permitted. The exact pictured variant is recorded below and in the JSON; it does not add a shade, model, size, flavor, edition, assortment-content or package-count promise to the unchanged seed label. Generic or unconfirmable bundles remain empty; no different-brand or approximate bundle substitutes are assigned.

Country trips use a verified location within the named country: Tokyo/Japan, Seoul/Korea, California/US, London/UK, Taipei/Taiwan, Sydney/Australia, Paris/France and Ha Long Bay/Vietnam. City trips use the actual city; Orchard Road has its own street photograph.

Actual web searches led to source pages and observed image links. Source-provided Wikimedia thumbnails were used when original downloads returned HTTP 429. There are no generated images, guessed asset identifiers, name-based image endpoints or search-result proxies.

## Validation

Run from the project root:

```sh
node --experimental-strip-types scripts/verify-catalog-images.mjs
```

The [read-only verifier](../scripts/verify-catalog-images.mjs) checks seed/manifest coverage and every occurrence, explicit missing strings, local paths, JPEG/PNG/WebP signatures and measured dimensions, SHA256 hashes, provenance, product watermark-free verification flags, cover ratios, square product files, and photo uniqueness across different product names. It makes no network requests or database writes. Content identity and absence of watermarks are visual inspections recorded in the manifest.
